Assumptions, in plain English

How we model the numbers.

Staff hours

Cenaris typically reduces ongoing compliance admin by 50–65% by eliminating manual evidence collection, version tracking, and search.

Audit prep

Prep windows shrink from weeks to days with mapped evidence and auditor read-only access. We model a 70% reduction.

Consultants

External spend drops as gap analysis and remediation move in-house. We model a 40% reduction year one.

Non-conformities

Each NC carries an estimated rectification + reputational cost of ~$3,500. We model a 60% reduction.

Subscription cost

We match staff count to our published plans: 1–10 staff → Tier 1 Essentials ($149/mo) · 11–50 → Tier 2 Assurance ($349/mo) · 51–150 → Tier 3 Confidence ($699/mo) · 151+ → Enterprise (from $1,499/mo). All plans billed annually.
