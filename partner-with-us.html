Partner FAQ

The fine print, in plain English.

What is the partner program? +
The Cenaris partner program allows someone to refer people or businesses to us to subscribe to the Cenaris platform. The referrer receives commission for a successful conversion.
How much can I earn? +
You can earn up to 15% of the total annual subscription, for the life of the subscription. This means recurring income for you, based upon the customer's subscription longevity. Commission is uncapped. You earn commission from your very first referral conversion to sale.
Does it cost anything to join? +
No cost to you — it is free.
How often are payments made? +
Payouts are calculated and paid annually, up front upon the successful first invoice being paid by the customer. Recurring commission is paid upon the renewal of each subscription, annually.
I've filled the form — what next? +
The Cenaris team will email you to confirm details and provide you the necessary details to refer and receive commission. We'll also provide you materials to share with potential customers — including specials and updates.
