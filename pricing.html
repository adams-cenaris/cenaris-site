Pricing will be announced closer to launch on 1 July 2026. We anticipate per-organisation pricing with tiers based on staff count and entity structure, billed annually. Final pricing and packaging will be shared with waitlist members first.
What do early-bird members get? +
Founding-member rates locked in for the duration of your subscription, priority onboarding from the Cenaris team, and a direct line to Adam during the first 90 days of your account.
Will there be a free trial? +
A trial period is planned; details to follow. Waitlist members will be notified first when trial terms are finalised.
