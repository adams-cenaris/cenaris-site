Why Cenaris exists

Australian compliance, made simple.

Cenaris is Australian founded, owned and operated. A compliance platform designed to help NDIS providers and healthcare organisations meet regulatory requirements with confidence, clarity, and less administrative burden.

We created Cenaris to solve a real problem: compliance in Australia is complex, time-consuming, and constantly changing. Too often, providers are forced to juggle spreadsheets, policies, evidence folders, and audit preparation across disconnected systems. This increases risk, stress, and the chance of non-compliance.

Our mission

To make Australian compliance clearer, safer, and more achievable for organisations that support people and communities.

Cenaris brings compliance obligations into one secure, easy-to-use platform that helps organisations understand what is required, what evidence is needed, and where gaps exist, before an audit or incident occurs.
